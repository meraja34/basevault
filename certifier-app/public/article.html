The Problem

Right now, document verification is broken:

  • A university issues a degree on paper. It can be forged, lost, or damaged.
  • A hospital stores medical records on a server. That server can go down, get hacked, or refuse access.
  • An auditor signs off on a report. Years later, no one can prove the original wasn't tampered with.
  • Cloud storage companies can delete your files, change terms, or shut down.

BaseVault fixes all of this by putting documents where no one can touch them: on the blockchain.

How It Works

1

Connect Wallet & Upload

Use any wallet (Coinbase, MetaMask, Rainbow) or the Android app's built-in wallet. Drop your file, choose public or private.

2

File Goes On-Chain

Entire file is split into 24KB chunks and stored directly in the Base smart contract. Not IPFS. Not a hash. The actual bytes.

3

Get It Certified

Registered institutions (universities, hospitals, law firms) issue on-chain certificates. 13 types supported, batch up to 100.

4

Anyone Can Verify

Drop a file on the verify page. SHA-256 auto-computed. All certifications shown. No wallet needed. No account needed.

Fully On-Chain File Storage

This is the core of BaseVault. When you upload a file, the entire file goes on-chain. Not a hash. Not a pointer. The actual bytes.

When you upload a 5MB PDF, all 5MB goes into the Base smart contract. It stays there forever. No one can take it down. Not us, not Base, not anyone.

Upload page - Files stored directly on Base chain, fully on-chain and permanent
  • Max file size: 50MB
  • Chunk size: 24KB (safe transaction limit)
  • Integrity: SHA-256 hash stored on-chain
  • Visibility: Public or private per file
  • Permanence: Once stored, cannot be deleted, modified, or censored by anyone. Ever.

AES-256-CTR Encryption

Private files get military-grade encryption before going on-chain. The encryption uses two-factor key derivation:

Key = SHA-256(wallet_signature + your_password)

Your wallet proves who you are. Your password adds a layer that even wallet theft can't break. Both are needed to decrypt. Neither is ever stored or transmitted.

Your encrypted files sit on a public blockchain, visible to everyone, but completely unreadable without both your wallet AND your password. Two things that only you have.

Institutional Certification

This is where BaseVault becomes more than just storage. Institutions can register on-chain and issue verifiable certificates.

13 Certification Types

Degree, Transcript, Course Completion, Professional License, Badge, Contract, NDA, Audit Report, IP Proof, Medical Record, Lab Report, Research Paper, and Generic.

Register as a verified issuer - On-Chain Trust, Batch Certification, Delegate Access

Key Features

  • Batch Certification: Certify up to 100 documents in a single transaction
  • Delegate System: Team members can issue certs on behalf of your institution
  • Revocation: Certificates can be revoked with an on-chain reason
  • Certify by Hash: Don't need to upload the file. Just hash it and certify. The document stays with you, the proof lives on-chain
  • Expiry Dates: Optional expiry for time-limited certifications
  • QR Codes: Every certificate gets a shareable page with QR code

The Android App

BaseVault has a standalone Android app (5.7 MB) that takes zero-server-dependency to the next level:

  • Built-in Local Wallet: Private key generated on your device, encrypted, never leaves your phone
  • Fingerprint Unlock: Biometric authentication for instant access
  • 5 RPC Failover: If one Base endpoint is down, app automatically tries 4 others
  • All Features: Upload, encrypt, certify, verify, browse, view certificates
  • No Backend: The app talks directly to the Base blockchain. Zero servers between you and your data
